About

I’m an applied mathematician and software developer. I’m interested in how mathematical models can help explain biological systems, especially neurons and brain function.

In my axon research, I used numerical methods to model how electrical signals travel along neurons. I also built a desktop app to simulate and visualize action potentials. My other academic projects include probability simulations, numerical methods, and tools for learning.

Outside of math and software, I enjoy reading, playing chess and music, playing basketball and volleyball, hiking, and rock climbing.
