All jobs Previous job Next job
Dates worked

Description

I loaded freight from conveyor belts into trailers, arranging boxes of different sizes into tight, stable loads to limit shifting in transit. I regularly worked 12.5-hour shifts and 48+ hour weeks. My supervisor had me train new hires on proper loading and stacking techniques. In addition, I worked in the Neighborhood Markets department, where I scanned individual packages and built pallets for forklift pickup. I tracked freight volume and the number of trailers our team closed.
