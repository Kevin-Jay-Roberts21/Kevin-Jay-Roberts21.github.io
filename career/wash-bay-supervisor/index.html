Wash Bay Supervisor

Ford Performance Racing School

Dates worked
May 2016 – August 2018
Employment type
Full-time, summer only

Description

I supervised the wash bay team and prepared vehicles for the driving school’s track sessions. I checked the quality of each cleaning, advised team members on which products and tools to use on different surfaces, and shuttled cars from the wash bay to the track. This included driving manual-transmission race cars and maneuvering them into tight parking spaces. I also managed fueling for the school fleet, making sure vehicles went to the track with full tanks, and I kept records of fuel receipts and how much fuel each vehicle received.
